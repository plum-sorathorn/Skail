Implementation notes (Textual / TCSS)

  • Nothing is boxed except the composer band. Panels are separated by border-top: solid $border and a single vertical border-left on the sidebar — so ATELIER is mostly padding + rules, which is cheap in TCSS and reads as print.
  • The transcript is a data grid, not cards. Each row is one Static/ListItem with four fixed-width children: width: 9 (clock), width: 2 (fold), width: 10 (role), then a flexible measure. Continuation lines inherit the measure offset for free.
  • Editorial signature = letterspaced small caps (text-style: bold + spaced content strings, since TCSS has no letter-spacing), tabular right-aligned costs (text-align: right in a fixed-width cell), and hairline rules.
  • Accent discipline: ochre $accent appears on exactly three things — the YOU role tag, the composer prompt/SEND, and the active tab underline. Everything else is bone/muted.
  • Budget is promoted out of its tab into the dateline (meter + %) and a permanent LEDGER block at the foot of the margin, so the 0.75/0.90 thresholds are visible without leaving the chat. The BUDGET tab keeps the full breakdown.
  • Approval becomes a full-bleed band with a 1-cell rubricated left margin (2px of $approval) instead of a round box; it is the only element with a tinted background, so it wins the attention race without shouting.
  • Narrow mode (width < 100): .right { display: none }, tabs collapse into a one-row drawer strip, the clock gutter hides (roles remain, so status is never colour-only), and the dateline wraps to two rows.
